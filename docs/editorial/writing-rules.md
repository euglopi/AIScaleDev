# Writing rules

Rules for everything AI Scale Dev writes: summaries, First look items, graded pages,
explainers and statements by leading voices. They apply to agents and people alike, and
the checker enforces the ones code can test.

## Voice and plain words

- The site writes as "we".
- Every unfamiliar term is explained in plain words first, with its real name after, and
  links to the glossary.

## Say only what was read

- A claim that something is absent ("the paper does not mention X") is made only when the
  whole source was read. Otherwise it says what was read: "in the sections we read".
- No list is called complete. The daily scan says how many sources were scanned, never
  "all the important news today". "No independent study found" becomes "No independent
  study found in the sources we scanned".
- A plural or general sentence ("teams find", "studies show") needs the count of cases
  behind it, or it is narrowed to what was checked ("in the study we read"). 1 checked case
  is not a count.

## Real people

- Only public, professional work and statements. Nothing about private life.
- A statement links to the person's own post, paper or recorded talk and is quoted
  exactly. Notes taken by someone else are labeled as notes until the person or a
  recording confirms them.
- "Did not come true" needs a dated source for the prediction and a dated source for the
  outcome.
- A page about a person, and any downgrade, waits for the owner's approval.
- When a person objects to how they or their work are shown, they are invited to correct
  the record or add context. A sourced fact stays; a correction with evidence changes it;
  their context appears beside it, labeled as their reply. A reply never changes an
  evidence grade without new evidence. A reply is posted only after checking it came from
  the person's known public account or address.

## Quoting and sources

- Summaries are in our own words and never stand in for the source.
- Quotes are short, exact and linked.
- A private copy of each source's text is kept only to check claims against. It is never
  republished.

## Charts

- A high-value chart may be copied when it is credited to its source.
- Proposed, not yet approved: record each chart's license, and when a license does not
  allow copying, redraw the chart from its numbers and credit the source.
